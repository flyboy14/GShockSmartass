# DW-H5600 fork of G-Shock Smart Sync

This fork (branch `h5600-sound-mode`) adds DW-H5600 (module 3516) features that the official
CASIO WATCHES app has, plus a few it does not. Protocol details: `docs/DW-H5600-protocol.md` in
the [GShockAPI fork](https://github.com/flyboy14/GShockAPI/tree/h5600-sound-mode).
Open research items: [`docs/DW-H5600-capture-checklist.md`](DW-H5600-capture-checklist.md).

## Building

The app is built against the **GShockAPI fork from source** (composite build), not JitPack.
Clone both repositories **side by side** and check out the branch in each:

```
git clone -b h5600-sound-mode git@github.com:flyboy14/GShockAPI.git
git clone -b h5600-sound-mode git@github.com:flyboy14/GShockSmartass.git
cd GShockSmartass
./gradlew testGithubDebugUnitTest assembleGithubDebug   # JDK 21, Android SDK platform 37
adb install -r app/build/outputs/apk/github/debug/app-github-debug.apk
```

`settings.gradle` includes `../GShockAPI` and substitutes `com.github.izivkov:GShockAPI` with it;
remove that block to go back to the published library.

Switching from the official app: unpair on the watch (SETTING → GENERAL SETTINGS → PAIRING →
UNPAIRING), forget the watch in Android's Bluetooth settings, then pair in this app. Only one app
can own the watch; keep the official app's Bluetooth permission revoked while using this one.

## Features

### Alarms tab (replaces the 4-slot editor)
- Any number of alarms, each with a **label**, **days** (any weekday combination), **Vibe only /
  Sound + vibe**, **snooze** (switch, off by default; slider 5/10/15/20/30 min, until stopped), and optional **smart** window (wake in light sleep up to 15/30/45/60
  min early). Hold and drag to reorder.
- The watch has 4 normal slots and 1 smart slot. The phone loads the alarms due in the next 24 h
  (marked "on watch" / "on watch (smart)") and swaps them as they ring; a snoozing alarm keeps its
  slot until its snooze is stopped. Alarms set on the watch itself are overwritten.
- **Snooze is the phone's**: the watch's own snooze is off on every slot and on the smart alarm
  (it stops after 7 x 5 min, only switching the alarm off ends it, and time syncs / slot writes
  cancel it). After an alarm with snooze rings, the phone rewrites its slot to the next repeat
  (ring + k x interval) a minute after each ring, without end, until *Stop snooze* in the
  notification or on the card (`AlarmPool.snoozing`, `slotTime`, `nextBoundary`, `SnoozeNotifier`).
  A smart alarm snoozes from its set time (in a normal slot) and can be stopped from the start of
  its window. Rings before the alarm was last set (`PhoneAlarm.since`, `AlarmPool.touch`) never
  snooze, and the next regular ring starts a new round. Time syncs skip ring minutes
  (`AlarmPool.ringing`).
- **Smart alarm** needs heart-rate monitoring All day or Activities + night (Sport tab); with Off it
  cannot detect light sleep.
- **Sound mode**: the watch stays in the mode you chose on the Custom tab. An alarm borrows it from
  5 min before it can ring (before a smart alarm's window) until the ring is over, or until its
  snooze is stopped from the phone; then your mode comes back.
  A vibe-only alarm gets VIBE ONLY; a sound alarm keeps your mode if it has sound, otherwise CUSTOM
  (`AlarmPool.desiredSoundMode`, `H5600Prefs.baseSoundMode`).

### Sport tab (heart rate and fitness, packets 0x41 / 0x43 / 0x4F / 0x13)
- **Heart-rate monitor**: All day / Activities + night / Off (0x41 bits 0–1). Off asks first while a
  smart alarm is on (it needs heart-rate monitoring to sense light sleep).
- **Heart-rate values**: max and resting heart rate, Auto (the watch's estimate) or your own value;
  VO2max as measured by the watch (0x4F).
- **Daily step goal** with − / + (500 steps), **goal notification**, **step reminder** (0x43, 0x13).

### Health tab
- Today's steps (ring with the watch's own goal), Hourly (24 h, scrollable, opens at the current
  hour), Daily (7 days) and Monthly (6 months), all on one screen.
- Steps come from the LifeLog sync, which only sends data the phone has not fetched yet. The app
  stores every sync (5-minute slots and daily totals, frame 0x63) and builds the charts from the
  store. Hours and days it has no data for are blank / "–", not 0.
- Cached for an hour, no background syncs from 00:00 to 06:00 (watch battery); pull to sync now. The exercise profile was removed: the watch accepts none.

### Custom tab
- **Watch face**: Face 1 (weekday) / Face 2; the face-2 item is Auto (Steps all day, Sun data from
  a set time before sunset until 10 min after), Sun, Steps, Moon, Heart rate or Sleep. Picking Heart
  rate asks to switch heart-rate monitoring to All day; leaving it asks to switch back.
- **Sun at home** (Auto only): on one of the saved home Wi-Fi networks the Auto face shows Sun all
  day; away, the steps / sunset logic. Wi-Fi changes are followed with a network callback
  (`HomeWifi`), so the face switches at once; networks are added from those in range (Android does not
  let apps read the saved-networks list). Needs precise location "Allow all the time" (the Wi-Fi name
  is hidden otherwise).
- **Sun & weather** (shown only for the Sun or Auto item; details collapsed): *Weather instead of sunrise* (the sun screen's top line shows `W:TT`, W = 1
  sunny, 2 cloudy, 3 rain, 4 extreme; TT = °C 0–59, from Open-Meteo hourly (not 00:00–06:00), sunset stays real;
  fake coordinates solved with the watch's own sun formula), with the real sunrise/sunset, the
  weather, what the watch should show and fine-tune buttons. The refresh icon fetches the weather
  and sends location and time to the watch (also done hourly and on every connection).
- **Sound & notifications**: the five sound modes (your choice is the mode the watch returns to after
  every alarm), **Silent at night** (the sound mode is Off — no sound, no vibration — from the start to
  the end of the night time, alarms excepted; phone-side, `AlarmPool.baseAt`), per-app notification filter.
- **Find Phone**: sound, volume, vibrate, test.

### Settings tab
- Button tone, auto light, illumination period, power saving (written on a fresh read).
- **Night time** (sleep time; 0x13 bytes 13–16 and 0x4F, written together; the phone keeps a copy).
- **Watch modes** (collapsed by default): the order of the watch's modes and which are shown
  (0x52). Hold and drag to reorder, switch off to hide, then Save — one write.

### Time tab
- Fine Adjustment; any number of extra clocks (one can be the watch's world time); watch 12/24 h
  (the whole app follows it); hourly time signal; battery as four pastel bars (red, orange, light
  green, green; an uncalibrated level is drawn lighter — tap to calibrate).

### Everywhere
- Explanations are behind the (i) button on each card; cards show values only.

### Removed / changed
- Actions tab, voice command, AUTO settings button, Locale card, exercise profile.
- Watch-sensor temperature is labelled as such (it reads body heat, not the weather).

## How it works (code map)

| Area | Files |
|---|---|
| Alarm list, slot planner, sound mode | `h5600/AlarmPool*.kt`, `h5600/AlarmEditor.kt`, `h5600/H5600ScheduleRunner.kt` |
| Custom tab | `h5600/CustomScreen.kt`, `h5600/H5600Card.kt`, `h5600/H5600ViewModel.kt` |
| Sport tab, Settings-tab cards (night time, watch modes), shared card parts | `h5600/H5600SettingsCards.kt` |
| Sun / weather, Sun at home | `h5600/SunFace.kt`, `h5600/TempSun.kt`, `h5600/HomeWifi.kt` |
| Notification filter | `h5600/NotificationFilter.kt`, `services/NotificationMonitorService.kt` |
| Steps and history store | `h5600/H5600Steps.kt`, `ui/health/*` |
| Time tab additions | `ui/time/TimeFormatRow.kt`, `ui/time/WorldClocksView.kt`, `ui/time/BatteryBars.kt` |
| App-wide 12/24 h, last known values | `utils/AppTimeFormat.kt`, `utils/LastKnown.kt` |
| Raw packet probe (debug builds) | `h5600/H5600ScheduleRunner.kt` (receiver), `tools/watch_raw.sh` |

`H5600ScheduleRunner.onConnected` runs on every connection (before the stock connection actions):
0x39 handshake, loc/time sync, alarm slots, smart slot, sound mode, watch face, weather; while
connected it wakes up at alarm boundaries, sunset window edges and hourly, except from 00:00 to 06:00 (every sync costs watch battery). At an alarm boundary it compares the plan with what it last read from or wrote to the watch and only contacts the watch when something has to change; it reads the watch again on every connection, every edit in the app, after a failure and at least every 6 hours.

How to add the next feature, and the patterns to reuse: [`DW-H5600-DEVELOPMENT.md`](DW-H5600-DEVELOPMENT.md).

## Tests

`./gradlew testGithubDebugUnitTest`: alarm planner and smart slot, sound-mode timing, day rules,
sunset maths, weather encoding (every W:TT for winter/summer/DST day), LifeLog replay of trimmed
captures (`app/src/test/resources/*.btsnoop`). Never commit full phone snoop logs: they contain
other devices and notification texts.

## Keeping up with upstream

- Weekly GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/sync-upstream.yml` in both forks) merge izivkov's
  `main`, run the tests and push only if they pass (needs Actions enabled, "Read and write"
  workflow permissions and `h5600-sound-mode` as default branch).
- Locally: `~/h5600-probe/sync-upstream.sh [--push]` (fetch, merge, test, build).

## Known limitations

- Per-function CUSTOM sound choices (0x42 bytes 2–5) are not mapped yet.
- Phone-side features (day rules, sound switching, slot swapping, weather) need the watch to be
  connected; the always-connected link usually is.
- Spring DST day: `1:TT` (sunny) cannot be shown (local 1:xx does not exist that night).
- Step history exists only from the first sync with this version on; the watch cannot re-send.
- Not supported by the watch (written, not kept): HR zone alert, date order, weekday language, font,
  operation vibration, auto pause, distance units, user profile.
